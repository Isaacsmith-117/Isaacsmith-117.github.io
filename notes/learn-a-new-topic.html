Learn a new topic

A selected learning-library note · Curated with AI assistance · Reviewed 2026-09-13

Start with a question you care about

“Learn more about AI” is broad. “How does a model use retrieved documents to answer a question?” has a finish line.

Here is a suggested exploration routine:

  1. Write the question and why the answer would help.
  2. Find a primary explanation: official documentation, an original paper, or a creator's account.
  3. Make a tiny example that tests your understanding.
  4. Look for a limitation or counterexample.
  5. Explain the result in your own words and link the source.
  6. Save one next question only if it follows naturally.

Know when to stop

Stop when you can answer the original question, show an example, and identify the limits of your answer. More tabs do not automatically make the explanation better.

A useful output

One short wiki page with a clear answer, an example, a source, and a related link. Label your own interpretation so that it does not become an unattributed fact.

This is an editorial workflow, not a measured claim about learning effectiveness.
